# FAQ page — section-by-section content

This page should answer the questions customers are likely to ask **before contacting a roofing supplier**. It should not turn unknown Wakatendeka policies into invented answers.

## Section 1 — Introduction

**Heading:** Questions about roofing materials and ordering.

**Text:** “Start here for answers about our products and how to contact us. For current sizes, colours, stock and prices, speak to our team.”

## Section 2 — Products and choosing

Include questions such as:

1. **What products does Wakatendeka sell?** Answer from the flyer: IBR Roofing Sheets, Q Tiles and Concrete Nails. Link to Products.
2. **What is the difference between IBR roofing sheets and Q Tiles?** Write a supplier-approved explanation of the actual products Wakatendeka carries; don't copy another company's product specifications.
3. **Which colours and sizes are available?** Explain that current options must be confirmed with Wakatendeka; add exact ranges only when provided by the business.
4. **How do I work out the quantity I need?** Ask for roof measurements or a plan and explain whether the company can help calculate quantities only if that service is confirmed.

## Section 3 — Quotes and ordering

5. **What information should I send for a quote?** Product name, dimensions if known, quantity, preferred colour, project location and a callback number.
6. **Are prices shown online?** Answer according to the chosen business policy; if prices change, state that a current quotation is needed.
7. **Can sheets be cut to a requested length?** Answer yes only if Wakatendeka confirms custom cutting and its limits.
8. **How do I place an order and pay?** Have Wakatendeka provide the actual order and payment steps before publishing.

## Section 4 — Collection, delivery and installation

9. **Where can I visit Wakatendeka?** Answer: 8 Swanesa Close, Nyakamete, Mutare. Add verified opening hours when supplied.
10. **Do you deliver?** Publish Wakatendeka's actual delivery areas, charges and lead times only when confirmed.
11. **Do you install roofs?** Clearly distinguish material supply from installation; answer only after the owner confirms the service.
12. **What happens if an item is damaged or incorrect?** Use the company's real returns and complaint process.
