# About page — research comparison and recommended content

The About page should explain **who Wakatendeka is, what it supplies, where it operates, and what a customer can genuinely expect**. Below is the requested comparison of ten Zimbabwean roofing and construction company About pages. These are their own claims, not facts about Wakatendeka.

## Comparison of ten About pages

| Company | What its About page actually covers | What Wakatendeka can learn |
|---|---|---|
| [Oryx Steel Industries](https://www.oryxsteel.co.zw/about-us) | Company identity and products, target projects, mission, vision, values, self-reported figures, and a contact link. | Start with a clear identity and offering; use statistics only when Wakatendeka can verify its own. |
| [Longni Hardware Supplies](https://longni.co.zw/about/) | Roofing materials, residential/commercial customers, mission, vision, objectives and contact information. | Identify the actual customers served, then explain the products they can buy. |
| [Roofing263](https://www.roofing263.co.zw/about-company/) | Mission, domestic/commercial/industrial audiences, roof profiles, accessories, cut-to-size work, and technical examples. | Say exactly what the business sells and does; distinguish products from services. |
| [Sunnyvan](https://www.sunnyvan.co.zw/about) | A concise company description, sheet and tile types, claimed sizing/colour range, accessories and a gallery link. | Product detail can make a short About page more useful than a generic long story. |
| [Aluconstruction Heritage](https://aluconstruction.co.zw/about-us/) | Product supply and contracting, mission, goals, reasons to choose, contact details. | If Wakatendeka only supplies products, do not imply it also installs roofs. |
| [Construction Solutions Africa](https://csafrica.co.zw/about/) | Who it is, specialist capabilities, how projects are managed, mission, vision and values. | Explain the actual customer process rather than relying only on “quality” and “trust.” |
| [Contigain Construction](https://contigain.co.zw/about-us/) | Founding, Mutare base, services, operating approach, mission/vision/values, compliance and contact. | Local roots and verifiable business details can be meaningful evidence. |
| [Tower Construction](https://towerconstruction.co.zw/about-us/) | Company history, story, vision, values, reasons to choose, claimed industry recognition and quotation link. | Give a true history if known, and finish with a direct next step. |
| [HIT Construction](https://www.hitconstruction.co.zw/about-us) | Founder and team, mission/vision/values, equipment, listed memberships, downloadable profile and quote route. | Use real people and credentials where they exist and can be substantiated. |
| [Redecon](https://redecon.co.zw/about/) | Team and leader, project approach, mission/vision, common questions and contact/quote paths. | Put a human face and practical answers alongside the company description. |

### Conclusion from the comparison

The clearest pattern is **identity → products/services → customer and operating approach → evidence → contact**. Roofing suppliers usually explain profiles, colours, sizes and accessories; construction contractors spend more space on teams, processes, equipment and completed projects. Wakatendeka’s supplied flyer establishes the brand, Mutare address and three product categories. Therefore its About page should primarily tell the story of a **roofing and building-supplies business**. Founding dates, manufacturing, installation, delivery coverage, certifications, testimonials and completed-project claims should be added only when Wakatendeka provides proof.

## Section 1 — Who we are

Open with a short factual introduction, not a generic slogan.

**Proposed wording for approval:** “Wakatendeka Roofing and Building Supplies is a roofing and building-materials business in Nyakamete, Mutare. We help customers enquire about IBR roofing sheets, Q Tiles and concrete nails for their projects.”

If the registered legal name, founding date and founder are provided, add them in a second paragraph. Otherwise do not guess.

## Section 2 — What we supply

Explain the three confirmed categories in one paragraph each. Say what an IBR sheet, Q Tile and concrete nail is for in general terms, then link to Products for Wakatendeka's real options. Do not list extra items simply because other suppliers sell them.

## Section 3 — Who we work with

State the actual customer groups: homeowners, builders, contractors and/or businesses **only after confirming** Wakatendeka serves them. Give one example of what each group might ask the team about, such as quantities or finish options. This makes the company description useful to the visitor.

## Section 4 — How we help a customer

Describe the real purchase journey: asking about a product, sharing measurements/quantity, confirming availability and quotation, then arranging collection or delivery **if offered**. This section should answer “What happens after I contact you?” rather than repeat vague values.

## Section 5 — The company story and people

Ask the owner for year founded, why the business started, who manages it, premises photos and staff photos. A short true story and named team are stronger than a fabricated timeline. If this information is unavailable, omit the story section at launch.

## Section 6 — Quality and trust evidence

Include evidence the owner can provide: actual product photos, specification sheets, manufacturer or supplier information, verified business registration or relevant memberships, approved customer feedback, and genuine examples of material supplied. Distinguish evidence from promotional claims. Do not invent “10+ years,” “1,000 projects,” warranties or certifications.

## Section 7 — Mission and values (only if approved)

If Wakatendeka wants a mission, make it specific to the business. **Draft, not an existing company statement:** “Help local customers choose and source roofing materials with clear product information and dependable communication.” Values should be tied to action: clear information, responsible product advice and honest quotations. Have the owner approve the final words.

## Section 8 — Where to find us

Close with **8 Swanesa Close, Nyakamete, Mutare**, **0771407179 / 0717154689**, and **wakatendekaroofing@gmail.com**. Invite the visitor to view Products or request a quote.

## Information Wakatendeka must supply before final About copy

Registered name; founding year and story; whether it manufactures or supplies; actual customer groups and service area; installation, custom cutting and delivery status; any real product guarantees; staff/owner photo permission; and proof behind quality claims.
