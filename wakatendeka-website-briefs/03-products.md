# Products page — section-by-section content

This page is the **catalogue overview**. Its job is to tell visitors what Wakatendeka stocks and let them move to a specific product or request current information. It should not pretend that competitor specifications describe Wakatendeka stock.

## Section 1 — Catalogue introduction

**Heading:** Roofing materials and building supplies.

**Text:** “Browse our main product categories and contact us for current sizes, colours, availability and prices.” This is a suitable summary of the three categories shown on Wakatendeka's flyer.

## Section 2 — IBR Roofing Sheets

Show an actual product image and explain what this category is: a ribbed metal roofing-sheet profile. State where customers commonly consider it, then list **only confirmed Wakatendeka options**: finish, gauge/thickness, sheet width, length, colours, and whether sheets can be cut to order. End with “View IBR details” and “Ask for current availability.”

## Section 3 — Q Tiles

Show the actual tile-profile sheet offered by Wakatendeka. Explain the tile-like appearance and list its **confirmed** finishes, gauges, colours, lengths and units of sale. Do not assume all Q Tiles shown on competing websites are the same as Wakatendeka’s stock. Link to “View Q Tile details.”

## Section 4 — Concrete Nails

Show a close-up of the actual packs and any size label. Describe the nail category and list confirmed sizes, finishes and pack quantities. Clarify intended use from a supplier-approved product sheet. Link to “View Concrete Nails details.”

## Section 5 — Choosing what to ask for

Prompt visitors to send the product name, approximate quantity, required dimensions, colour preference and project location. If they do not know the specification, say “Contact us with a photo or measurements and we can discuss the available options.” Do not promise design or engineering advice without confirmation.

## Section 6 — Other products, if stocked

Add accessories such as ridges, gutters, timber or roofing fasteners **only if Wakatendeka confirms the exact range**. Keep this section absent if the flyer’s three categories are the full launch catalogue.

## Section 7 — Product enquiry

Close with: “Need a particular size or colour? Send your requirements and ask for current stock and pricing.” Link to Contact/Quote with the chosen category included.

## What the research contributed

[Sunnyvan](https://www.sunnyvan.co.zw/products) separates IBR, Q Tile and accessories; [Longni](https://longni.co.zw/products/) lists clear product categories; [Altcom](https://altcom.co.zw/store/categories) offers a broader catalogue; [Roofing263](https://www.roofing263.co.zw/about-company/) shows detailed profile information. The conclusion is to begin with Wakatendeka’s three verified categories and make each one informative before expanding.
