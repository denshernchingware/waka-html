# Home page — section-by-section content

The home page should give a new visitor four answers quickly: **what Wakatendeka sells, who it serves, why to enquire, and how to contact it**. The sections below are in recommended reading order.

## Section 2 — The three main product categories

Introduce exactly the three categories shown in Wakatendeka's flyer:

1. **IBR Roofing Sheets:** “View IBR roofing sheets and ask about current sizes, finishes and colours.”
2. **Q Tiles:** “Explore tile-profile roofing options and ask which colours and sizes are available.”
3. **Concrete Nails:** “Ask about available nail sizes and pack quantities for your project.”

Each category should link to its own product information. Do not state particular measurements, colours, prices or stock levels until Wakatendeka supplies them.

## Section 3 — What kinds of customers this helps

Explain the intended customer groups in plain language: a homeowner roofing a house, a builder sourcing materials, or a contractor planning a larger order. The text can say: “Whether you are building, renovating or sourcing materials for a client, start by telling us what product and quantity you need.” Confirm that all these customer groups are actually served before publishing the final wording.

## Section 4 — Why contact Wakatendeka

This section should translate the flyer’s claims into useful information. The flyer says **durable quality**, **affordable prices** and **reliable supply**. Under each heading, explain the actual evidence Wakatendeka can show:

- **Durable quality:** product specifications, manufacturer details, or material grades.
- **Affordable prices:** current quotation process and what affects price; no blanket “cheapest” promise.
- **Reliable supply:** current stock, ordering process, and any confirmed collection or delivery arrangements.

Until that supporting information is available, use modest copy such as “Ask our team about the right materials for your project.”

## Section 5 — How an enquiry works

Keep this about the visitor's actions, without promising unconfirmed company policies:

1. **Choose a product** from IBR sheets, Q Tiles or concrete nails.
2. **Share your requirements**: quantity, measurements if known, colour if relevant, and where the project is located.
3. **Contact the team** to confirm options and receive a quotation.

## Section 6 — Products in real use

Show a small preview of product or customer photographs. Each caption should say what is actually pictured: for example, “Blue IBR roofing sheet” rather than “Wakatendeka completed this installation” unless that is documented. Link to the Gallery page for more examples. If there are no genuine photos yet, omit this section until they are available.

## Section 7 — Common questions

Show short links to three questions: “Which roofing profiles do you sell?”, “How do I request a quote?”, and “Where is the shop?” The full answers belong on the FAQ page. Only publish extra questions about delivery, installation or custom cutting after the company confirms its policy.

## Section 8 — Final contact invitation

**Heading:** Ready to plan your roof?

**Text:** “Tell us which materials you need and we’ll help you with the next step.” Provide the flyer’s contact details: **0771407179 / 0717154689**, **8 Swanesa Close, Nyakamete, Mutare**, and **wakatendekaroofing@gmail.com**. Confirm which number supports WhatsApp before labeling it a WhatsApp contact.

## Why these sections

Zimbabwean roofing sites commonly lead with specific products and a clear enquiry route: [Roofstar](https://roofstar.co.zw/) shows products and quotation, [Altcom](https://altcom.co.zw/) shows product choices and reasons to enquire, and [Roofing263](https://www.roofing263.co.zw/about-company/) explains products by customer type. Wakatendeka's version should stay within its own confirmed product range and promises.
