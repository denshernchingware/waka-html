# Contact and quote page — section-by-section content

The contact page should give a customer two simple options: **contact Wakatendeka directly** or **send enough details for a product quotation**.

## Section 1 — Invitation to contact

**Heading:** Tell us what your project needs.

**Text:** “Ask about IBR roofing sheets, Q Tiles or concrete nails. If you know the dimensions and quantity, include them so our team can respond more usefully.”

## Section 2 — Direct contact details

Use the details visible on the supplied Wakatendeka flyer:

- **Phone:** 0771407179 / 0717154689.
- **Email:** wakatendekaroofing@gmail.com.
- **Visit:** 8 Swanesa Close, Nyakamete, Mutare, Zimbabwe.

State verified shop opening hours and directions when the owner provides them. If one number is WhatsApp-enabled, name that specific number after confirmation.

## Section 3 — Request a quotation

Introduce the enquiry form with: “Tell us which product you need and what you already know about your project. We can then confirm the available options and current price.” Collect:

1. Name and a way to reply (phone or email).
2. Product category: IBR Roofing Sheets, Q Tiles, Concrete Nails, or “Other enquiry.”
3. Quantity and measurements, if known.
4. Colour or finish preference, if relevant.
5. Project location.
6. A free-text explanation or question.
7. Optional roof plan/photo only if the business has a way to receive files safely.

Do **not** promise a response time, free quotation, delivery date or fixed price until Wakatendeka confirms those terms.

## Section 4 — What happens after an enquiry

Describe the **real business process** once confirmed. Suggested structure for the owner to complete: “We review your product requirements → contact you to clarify sizes or availability → send a quotation → explain collection or delivery options if offered.” This is a draft process, not a verified existing policy.

## Section 5 — Visit the shop

Repeat the Mutare address, add a verified map pin and any practical directions or landmark supplied by the owner. If opening hours are unknown, invite the visitor to call first rather than guessing hours.

## Section 6 — Final reassurance

Close with a short sentence: “Not sure which product to ask for? Tell us about your project and the team can discuss what Wakatendeka has available.” Keep the invitation modest; don't imply structural design advice or installation expertise without confirmation.

## What the research contributed

[Roofstar](https://roofstar.co.zw/) has a quotation form, [Tower Construction](https://towerconstruction.co.zw/about-us/) repeatedly offers a quotation route, and [Oryx Steel](https://www.oryxsteel.co.zw/about-us) closes its company explanation with contact. Wakatendeka's page should likewise make the next step obvious, while collecting details that actually help it reply.
