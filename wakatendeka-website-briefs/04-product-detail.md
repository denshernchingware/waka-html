# Product detail page — content template

Use this same page structure for **IBR Roofing Sheets**, **Q Tiles** and **Concrete Nails**, with different facts and photos for each. The catalogue page introduces categories; a detail page answers questions about one actual item.

## Section 1 — Name and short explanation

Show the precise item name and one sentence describing it. Example for the IBR page: “IBR Roofing Sheets — ask Wakatendeka about the finishes, lengths and colours currently available.” Use a real photo of Wakatendeka's product. Avoid presenting a generic rendered house as a product photograph.

## Section 2 — Available options

List only confirmed variants. For sheets or tiles: profile, material/finish, gauge, width, length, colour and unit of sale. For nails: size, finish, pack quantity and intended application. Where an attribute is not yet supplied by Wakatendeka, omit it; do not insert values from another company.

## Section 3 — Where the product may be used

Explain common uses in plain language, then make clear that suitability for a particular roof depends on the project specification. A supplier or qualified installer should verify technical choices. This section helps visitors understand the product without promising that every variant fits every project.

## Section 4 — What to confirm before ordering

Give a short checklist relevant to the item. For roofing sheets: measurements, quantity, profile, colour, related accessories, collection or delivery arrangement. For concrete nails: nail size, quantity and suitability for the application. This list should help a visitor send a useful enquiry.

## Section 5 — Product facts and documents

Publish a Wakatendeka-approved specification table or downloadable manufacturer sheet if available. It can include exact dimensions, material, finish, care instructions and warranty terms. Identify the manufacturer and date of the information if known. Do not create unsupported warranties, thicknesses or cover widths.

## Section 6 — Related items

Show only relevant products Wakatendeka genuinely stocks. For example, the flyer confirms concrete nails and two roofing-sheet categories; whether a particular nail is suitable for a particular roof assembly must be checked rather than assumed.

## Section 7 — Ask about this product

End with a contact action carrying the product name and any selected options. Suggested wording: “Tell us the quantity and dimensions you need; we’ll confirm current options and pricing.”

## Research basis

[Roofing263](https://www.roofing263.co.zw/about-company/) provides roofing profile explanations and technical examples, [Sunnyvan](https://www.sunnyvan.co.zw/products) presents different product types, and [Unitop](https://unitop.co.zw/our-services/) separates sheets, tiles and accessories. The page structure borrows the **type of information** those sites provide, never their values as Wakatendeka facts.
