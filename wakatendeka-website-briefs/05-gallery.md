# Gallery page — section-by-section content

The Gallery page should help people see product appearance and choose a look. It should **not** imply Wakatendeka built or installed a roof unless that role is documented.

## Section 1 — Introduction

**Heading:** Roofing inspiration and product photos.

**Text:** “See roofing profiles, colours and examples to help you decide what to ask us about.” If the gallery includes customer installations, explain whether Wakatendeka supplied materials, installed the roof, or simply has permission to share the image.

## Section 2 — Our product photos

Show actual photographs of Wakatendeka’s IBR Roofing Sheets, Q Tiles and Concrete Nails. Every caption names the product. Where a finish/colour is known, state it accurately. Link each image to the related product detail page.

## Section 3 — Colours and finishes

Group photos by the **real colours and finishes currently available**. A photograph is helpful but colour on screens varies; add a short note asking customers to confirm physical samples or stock colours before ordering. Do not list a colour simply because it appears on another roofing company's website.

## Section 4 — Customer projects, only with evidence

If Wakatendeka has permission to show a project, give each example a small factual caption:

- Type of property or roof.
- Product actually supplied by Wakatendeka.
- Colour/profile if confirmed.
- Whether installation was done by Wakatendeka, by a customer, or by a separate contractor.
- Location and client name only with permission.

If these facts are unavailable, keep the page as a **product gallery**, not “Our completed projects.”

## Section 5 — Help me get a similar look

For each project/product example, let the visitor ask about that exact material: “I’m interested in this roofing style.” The enquiry should include the product or gallery caption, then request their dimensions and approximate quantity.

## Section 6 — Contact invitation

**Text:** “Have a photo of the look you want? Contact Wakatendeka and tell us what material, colour and quantity you are considering.” Confirm that receiving images by email or a chosen messaging channel is operational before promising it.

## What the research contributed

[Longni](https://longni.co.zw/our-projects/) has project and colour galleries; [Sunnyvan](https://www.sunnyvan.co.zw/gallery) groups examples by roof use; [Contigain](https://contigain.co.zw/projects-clients/) identifies actual projects and clients. For Wakatendeka, this supports clear captions and a strict distinction between **inspiration**, **materials supplied**, and **work performed**. Generated images can be used for inspiration only if labeled as illustrative.
